How to Buy

Join the colony in four steps

You're on Ethereum

$ANT lives on Ethereum mainnet. You'll need an Ethereum wallet and some ETH for the swap and gas fees.

  1. 01

    Get MetaMask

    Install the MetaMask app or browser extension from metamask.io and create a wallet. Keep your seed phrase private.

  2. 02

    Buy ETH

    Buy ETH inside MetaMask or on an exchange, then send it to your MetaMask wallet address.

  3. 03

    Open Uniswap

    Go to app.uniswap.org and connect your MetaMask wallet.

  4. 04

    Paste CA & swap

    Paste the official contract address (TBA), choose an amount of ETH and confirm the swap.
